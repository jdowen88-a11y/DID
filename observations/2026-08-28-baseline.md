# Updated Observation Baseline — 2026-08-28

DID now treats Fire, Earth, Water, Air, and Ether as simultaneous channels in one shared field.

Observation, symbolic interpretation, neural distribution, learned weights, memory, links, and output remain connected. Scores may describe relative activation without electing an authorized speaker.

## Five-position / five-channel seed

- five channels present
- no predetermined winner
- no background caste
- no ejection authority
- no qualification step
- relationships may emerge through resonance
- manual amplification may change volume without muting another channel

## Learning rule

Every experience is available to every elemental trainer through its own lens. No focus winner owns the event.

## External effects

The local API is invoked explicitly. Internal expression requires no approval gate; an outside action still requires an explicit caller.

This branch inherits the current simultaneous-field implementation from `main` so its observation notes cannot sit on top of the old single-speaker engine.
